ANTI-BULLYING Act (RA 10627)

Bullying is any severe or repeated physical, verbal, written, electronic, or social behavior by one or more students that causes or may cause physical or emotional harm, creates a hostile school environment, violates another student's rights, or disrupts learning and school operations. Anyone who commits these acts is considered a bully. Retaliating against someone who reports bullying, participates in an investigation, witnesses bullying, or has reliable information about it is also prohibited.

The Lagronian Way

At Lagro High School, we strive for excellence in both academics and character. We celebrate and honor one another by practicing Self-discipline, Modesty, Academic excellence, Resiliency, and Trustworthiness (SMART). We always give our best whenever in or out of school, embodying these values. This is who we are, even when no one is watching.

  • M - Modesty
  • A - Academic excellence
