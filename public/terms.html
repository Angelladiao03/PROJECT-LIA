LagroInAction

Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Example draft, version draft-2026-10. To be reviewed and approved by the research adviser and the Lagro High School Guidance Office before real students use LagroInAction.

Please read this before creating an account. It explains what LagroInAction is, the rules for using it, and what happens to the information you give us. If something is unclear, ask the Guidance Office.

1. What LagroInAction is

LagroInAction ("LIA") is a website of Lagro High School where students can report bullying to the Guidance Office, follow what happens to their report, send an SOS alert, and message guidance staff. It was developed by Grade 12 students as a research project, with the Guidance Office.

LIA is an extra way to ask for help. It does not replace talking to a teacher, adviser, guidance counselor, or your parents or guardian.

2. Who can use it

  • Students of Lagro High School.
  • Guidance staff who have their own staff account.

3. Guest accounts

  • A guest account only needs your email address. We do not ask for your name.
  • You get a guest ID (like LIA-7K3P9Q) and a password. Keep your password secret and do not share your account.
  • A guest account lasts 30 days. If you have a report that is still being handled, or a request to become a student account, it stays open until that is finished.
  • When a guest account expires, you can no longer sign in to it, but your reports are kept by the Guidance Office so they can still act on them.

4. Permanent student accounts

  • You can ask to turn your guest account into a permanent student account. You will give your full name, grade and section, adviser, contact number, and a photo of your school ID.
  • Only Guidance Office staff see the ID photo, and only to check that you are a Lagro High School student. The photo is deleted as soon as they approve or decline your request.
  • We email you the result. If it is declined, the email says why.

5. Reports and anonymity

  • Guest reports never show who sent them. Staff only see the report itself.
  • Student accounts can choose "Hide my name from staff". The report is still linked to your account in the system so you can follow it in My reports, but staff cannot see your name.
  • Even when your name is hidden, the details you write can sometimes show who you are. Write what is needed, and be careful with names of other people.
  • If a report shows that someone is in serious danger, the school must act under the Anti-Bullying Act (RA 10627), its implementing rules, and the DepEd Child Protection Policy. This may include contacting parents or guardians, or the authorities, for your safety.

6. Rules for using LIA

  • Tell the truth. False or prank reports and SOS alerts waste help that someone else may need, and may be handled under school rules.
  • Use SOS only when you are in danger or need help right away.
  • Be respectful in messages. Do not post threats, insults, or other people's private information.
  • Do not try to sign in to someone else's account or break the website.

7. What information we collect, and why

  • Email address: to verify your account and send you important notices.
  • What you write in reports, SOS alerts, and messages: so the Guidance Office can help.
  • Student account details (name, grade and section, adviser, contact number): so staff can reach you about your report.
  • Security records (when you signed in, failed sign-in attempts, and the network address used): to protect accounts from people guessing passwords.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We only use it for the purposes above and for the research study, where results are reported only as totals that do not identify anyone.

8. Who can see your information

  • Authorized Lagro High School Guidance Office staff, using their own accounts.
  • The student researchers who built LIA use only made-up test data. They do not have access to real reports.
  • Others only when the law requires it, as described in part 5.

9. How long we keep it

  • Reports and messages are kept as school records for as long as the school's records policy requires. Staff can archive old reports, but they are not deleted.
  • ID photos are deleted right after review.
  • Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes.

10. Your rights under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

You have the right to be informed, to access your information, to ask us to correct it, to object, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights, contact the Guidance Office.

11. For parents and guardians

Most users are minors. Parents and guardians may contact the Guidance Office with questions about LIA and how student information is handled.
