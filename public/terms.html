Please read this notice carefully before creating an account. It explains what LagroInAction is, the rules for using it, and how the information you provide is collected, used and protected in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). If any part is unclear, please ask the Guidance Office before you continue.

Summary

  • LagroInAction is used to report bullying to the Guidance Office, follow the status of your report, send an SOS alert, and message guidance staff.
  • Guest reports never show your name. Students may choose to submit anonymously; staff then see only your grade level and mobile number.
  • Only authorized guidance staff can view reports, each using their own account, and their actions are recorded.
  • Your information is used only to respond to reports and keep you safe. It is never sold or used for advertising.
  • LagroInAction is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest teacher or security guard.

1. About LagroInAction

LagroInAction ("LIA") is a web-based bullying incident reporting and case management system of Lagro High School. Through LIA, students may report bullying to the Guidance Office, follow the progress of their reports, send an SOS alert, and communicate with guidance staff. LIA was developed by Grade 12 students as a research project, in cooperation with the Guidance Office.

LIA is an additional way to ask for help. It does not replace talking to a teacher, an adviser, a guidance counselor, or your parents or guardian, and it does not replace guidance sessions.

2. Who may use LagroInAction

  • Students of Lagro High School, through a guest account or a permanent student account.
  • Authorized Guidance Office personnel and school officials, each through their own staff account. Staff accounts are never shared.

3. Guest accounts

  • A guest account requires only your email address and mobile number. Your name is not asked.
  • You will receive a Guest ID (for example, LIA-7K3P9Q) and a password. Keep your password private and do not let anyone else use your account.
  • A guest account is valid for 30 days. It remains open while a report or a request for a student account is still being handled.
  • When a guest account expires, you can no longer sign in to it, but your reports are kept by the Guidance Office so that they can still be acted upon.

4. Permanent student accounts

  • You may request to change your guest account into a permanent student account by providing your full name, grade level and section, adviser, and a photo of your school ID. The mobile number of your guest account is kept.
  • The photo of your school ID is viewed only by Guidance Office staff, only to confirm that you are a Lagro High School student. It is deleted as soon as your request is approved or declined.
  • The result of your request is sent to your email address. If it is declined, the reason is stated.
  • Your section is confirmed when the request is approved. To change your full name, email address or section afterwards, please contact the Guidance Office.

5. Reports and anonymity

  • Guest reports are always anonymous. Staff see the report, the grade level you selected, and your mobile number only, so that they can contact you if necessary.
  • Students may choose to include their identity or to submit anonymously. An anonymous report is still linked to your account so that you can follow it in My Report History, but staff cannot see your name; they see only your grade level and mobile number. In this notice, "anonymous" means that your identity is hidden from staff.
  • When you include your identity, staff can see your full name, grade level and section, adviser, and mobile number, so that they can follow up with you directly.
  • Each report is handled by the guidance advocate of its grade level and by the Guidance Counselor. Your personal details are shown to them only after they re-enter their own password, and every viewing is recorded.
  • Even when your identity is hidden, the details you write may sometimes reveal who you are. Write only what is needed, and be careful with the personal details of other people.
  • Reports are never deleted. Staff may archive a report once it is resolved or not accepted, and every change to a report is recorded in its case history.
  • If a report shows that someone is in serious danger, the school is required to act under the Anti-Bullying Act of 2013 (RA 10627) and its implementing rules, and the DepEd Child Protection Policy. This may include informing parents or guardians, or the proper authorities, for the safety of the persons involved.

6. SOS alerts and emergencies

  • An SOS alert sends your location in the school and a short description to the Guidance Counselor and the guidance advocates, together with your mobile number.
  • Use SOS only when you are in danger or need help right away.
  • LIA depends on an internet connection and on staff being available, so it cannot guarantee an immediate response. In an emergency, call 911, or go to the nearest teacher, guard or school official.

7. Messages and files

  • Messages are private between you and authorized guidance staff. They are kept as part of the school's records of the case.
  • You may edit or delete your own messages. Deleted messages are hidden from view, and earlier versions of edited messages are kept for the record.
  • After a report is resolved or not accepted, the Guidance Office may close its conversation. A closed conversation can still be read, and it may be reopened by the Guidance Office.
  • Files sent in messages are limited to PDF, Word, JPG and PNG files of up to 5 MB.

8. Rules of use

  • Provide true and accurate information. False or prank reports and SOS alerts take help away from someone who may need it, and may be handled under school rules.
  • Be respectful in messages. Do not send threats, insults, or other people's private information.
  • Do not sign in to another person's account, share your own account, or attempt to disrupt or break the website.
  • Violations of these rules may be referred to the school for appropriate action.

9. Information we collect and why

  • Email address: to verify your account and to send important notices, such as updates on your report and unread messages.
  • Mobile number: so that the Guidance Office can contact you about your report or SOS alert, even when your identity is hidden.
  • Contents of reports, SOS alerts and messages: so that the Guidance Office can respond and help.
  • Student account details (full name, grade level and section, adviser, mobile number): so that staff can follow up on reports that include your identity.
  • Security records (sign-in times, failed sign-in attempts, and the network address used): to protect accounts from unauthorized access.

Your information is never sold or used for advertising. It is used only for the purposes above and for the research study, in which results are reported only as totals that do not identify anyone.

10. Who can see your information

  • Guidance Counselor: all reports, SOS alerts and conversations.
  • Guidance advocates: reports and conversations of their own grade level, and all SOS alerts.
  • Principal: totals and charts only, without names or case details.
  • The student researchers who developed LIA use only made-up test data and have no access to actual reports.
  • Other persons or offices only when required by law, as described in part 5.

11. Email notifications

  • You will receive an email when the status of your report changes, when you have unread messages from the Guidance Office, and for account matters such as verification codes and password resets.
  • Guidance staff receive an email when a new report or SOS alert is submitted, so that they can respond promptly.
  • For your privacy, emails never contain the description of a report, the contents of messages, or the name of the reporter.
  • LIA will never ask for your password by email. Emails from LIA are automatically generated; please do not reply to them.

12. How your information is protected

  • The website is accessed only through a secure (HTTPS) connection.
  • Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be read, even by staff.
  • Each staff member has their own account, can access only what their role requires, and every action is recorded with their name and the time.
  • Accounts are signed out after a period of no activity, and are temporarily locked after repeated wrong passwords.

13. How long information is kept

  • Reports, SOS alerts and messages are kept as school records for as long as the school's records policy requires. They may be archived, but they are not deleted.
  • Photos of school IDs are deleted right after the request is reviewed.
  • Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes, and password reset links after 30 minutes.

14. Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to be informed about how your information is used, to access it, to have it corrected, to object to its processing, to ask for its erasure or blocking (subject to the school's duty to keep case records), to data portability, to claim damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise any of these rights, please contact the Guidance Office.

15. For parents and guardians

Most users of LIA are minors. Parents and guardians are welcome to contact the Guidance Office with questions about LIA and about how student information is handled.
