Create a guest account

A guest account only needs your email address. Guidance staff will not see your name on your reports. You can still check your reports and message the guidance office.

Guest accounts last 30 days. Your reports are kept even after that.

How to use LagroInAction

  1. Report anything that happened to you or that you saw. Guest reports never show who sent them.
  2. My reports shows what the guidance office is doing with each report.
  3. Messages lets you talk with guidance staff privately.
  4. Tap the red SOS button if you are in danger right now.
  5. Want a permanent account? Go to Account and ask to become a student account.
