Guest accounts are valid for 30 days. Your reports are retained by the Guidance Office after that period.

How to use LagroInAction

  1. Submit Report: report any bullying incident that happened to you or that you witnessed. Guest reports are anonymous.
  2. My Report History: monitor the status of each report you submitted.
  3. Message: communicate privately with the Guidance Office.
  4. SOS: press the red SOS button if you are in immediate danger.
  5. My Account: request a permanent student account.
